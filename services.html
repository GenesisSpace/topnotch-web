FAQs

Common Questions

Can I order a single service on its own?+

Yes. Every service is independent. You can order consumables, office supply, uniforms, printing or any other service separately, or combine several into one project.

Do you work with government and institutional tenders?+

Yes. We are a registered, compliant supplier experienced in tender preparation, bidding and contract execution for government and institutional clients.

Can Topnotch Inc. handle multi-service projects?+

Yes — our integrated structure means one project can combine supply, technical, construction and consultancy support under a single point of contact.

Can you brand uniforms and print materials with our logo?+

Yes. We supply corporate wear, staff uniforms and promotional clothing, and we print business cards, banners, flyers and brochures with your branding.

Do you provide services outside Dar es Salaam?+

Yes, we deliver supply and project support nationwide, coordinated from our head office.

How do I request a quote?+

Use our contact form, call our office, or message us on WhatsApp with your requirement — our team will respond within one business day.
